London AI School is an independent, project-driven environment where people learn AI by building real systems, and progress toward research and open exploration as projects mature. Work is guided primarily by personalised AI mentor agents, with human experts involved selectively at critical decision points for calibration and direction.

What this is

  • Project-driven work on building, researching, and exploring advanced AI systems, agents, reasoning, and evaluation.
  • Personalised AI mentor agents provide continuous guidance, critique, and iteration support throughout the project lifecycle.
  • Human experts are involved selectively at key milestones and high-stakes decision points for calibration and direction.

What this is not

  • Not a degree-awarding or accredited institution.
  • Not a bootcamp, course platform, or credentialing programme.
  • Not a guided introduction to AI fundamentals.

Who it’s for

People from any background who want to learn AI by building real systems, and are willing to work through ambiguity, iteration, and rigorous feedback as projects develop toward research and exploration.
